Privacy policy

iwanta.cat is a free, open-source website without accounts, tracking, analytics or advertising. This page explains which data is processed when you use it.

Your location

Your location is only used after you click “Use my location” or search for a place. The list of animal shelters is downloaded as a whole and the nearest shelters are calculated in your browser – your location is not sent to us.

External services

Your browser connects directly to the following services. Like any web server, they receive your IP address.

Hosting – GitHub Pages

Provider
GitHub, Inc.
Purpose
Delivering this website
Data
IP address, requested pages, browser information (server logs)
Privacy policy
GitHub Privacy Statement

Map tiles – OpenStreetMap

Provider
OpenStreetMap Foundation
Purpose
Displaying the map
Data
IP address, the map area you view
Privacy policy
OSMF Privacy Policy

Place search – Nominatim

Provider
OpenStreetMap Foundation
Purpose
Finding the coordinates of a place you search for
Data
IP address, your search text
When
Only when you use the search field
Privacy policy
OSMF Privacy Policy

Approximate location – geojs.io

Provider
geojs.io
Purpose
Estimating your location from your IP address
Data
IP address
When
Only after you click “Use my location” and your browser cannot determine your position (this is not used if you deny location access)
Privacy policy
geojs.io Privacy Policy

Fallback shelter search – Overpass API

Providers
overpass-api.de (FOSSGIS e.V.), overpass.private.coffee
Purpose
Searching OpenStreetMap for animal shelters near a location
Data
IP address, the searched location
When
Only if the prebuilt shelter list cannot be loaded

External links

Links to directions (Google Maps), OpenStreetMap or shelter websites open the respective third-party site, whose own privacy policy applies.

Local storage

No cookies are used. Your browser’s local storage remembers your colour theme and – if you used “Use my location” – that your location should be looked up automatically on your next visit. This data never leaves your device.

Questions or concerns? Please open an issue on GitHub.
